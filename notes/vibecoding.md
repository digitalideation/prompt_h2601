# VibeCoding: facilitation notes from the previous semester

The original student activity is preserved in [the activity guide](../activities/vibecoding.md). It is not part of the planned H26 evaluation clinic.

- Introduce the reference artwork and discuss rhythm, repetition and minimalism.
- Demonstrate a starter sketch and how changing numbers affects the result.
- Walk through account setup and copying the template.
- Invite verbal descriptions such as slower, denser or brighter before changing parameters.
- Queue the submitted frame pairs for the video demonstration and compare the results.
