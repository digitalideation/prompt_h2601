Country: Kalmar Union
Title:O Union!
Story: Born from a historic alliance across frozen archipelagos, the Kalmar Union is a nation of fierce seafarers and daring explorers. Rooted in rich Viking tradition, its people brave treacherous icy waters to map unknown horizons, bound by a shared oath of unity, endurance, and relentless discovery.   A dramatic coastal landscape of a Norwegian fjord with traditional Scandinavian timber houses and dramatic snow-capped mountains reflecting in the cold water 
Model: minimax/music-2.6
Prompt: Epic Nordic folk, 68 BPM. Features a driving frame drum oar rhythm, tagelharpa, deep cello, and swelling horns. Melancholic yet heroic, transitioning from a solitary wind-swept intro to a powerful, sea-faring orchestral climax.
