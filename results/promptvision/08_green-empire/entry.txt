Country: The Green Empire
Title: The Ground Is Listening
Story: Humanity is gone, but nothing was forgotten. Every life is
  stored in tree rings and passed through the root network like a
  signal. The plants are rebuilding what they remember: cities grown
  instead of built, a government of ancient oaks. They imitate us
  perfectly. They are also preparing for something we could never
  survive.
Model: minimax/music-2.6
Prompt: Dark hypnotic organic techno, 124 BPM, F minor. Opens with
  creaking wood, rustling leaves, dripping water and a low sub-bass
  pulse like roots moving under concrete. Cold, close, breathy female
  vocal, half-whispered, calm and detached. Tight percussion built
  from wood knocks, snapping branches and log drums, locked over a
  heavy four-on-the-floor kick. Deep rolling bassline, dark analog
  synth stabs, glassy arpeggios flickering like light through leaves.
  The drop hits around 40 seconds: full kick, massive sub-bass and a
  layered choir chanting the hook. Minimal, menacing, cinematic,
  serious and restrained. Ends with everything cutting out except one
  deep wooden creak.
Translation: Vines strangling the skyscrapers became wooden percussion
  locked over a rigid four-on-the-floor kick, organic against machine;
  green light filtering through the canopy became glassy, flickering
  arpeggios and an airy choir; giant trees dwarfing the ruins became
  massive sub-bass and a slow, relentless build.
Iteration: Our first version was slow instrumental dark ambient:
  atmospheric but static. We kept the creaking-wood sound design and
  built it into hypnotic techno with a beat, a cold vocal and a drop.
Lyrics: Hush. The ground is listening.
  Roots in the wires, moss on the screens,
  your cities sleeping under fields of green.
  We read your summers in the rings of oak,
  every name you carved, every word you spoke.
  Quiet now, we're almost grown.
  We remember you, we remember you.
  Grown, not built.
  We are the cities now, we are the cities now.
  Something is rising, slow and wide,
  something you will not survive.
  (Chorus repeats)
  The ground is listening.
