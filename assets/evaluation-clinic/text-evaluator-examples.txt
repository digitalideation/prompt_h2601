Community Plant Swap

FOR DISCUSSION (check your own conclusions first)
- The baseline preserves the supplied time and location.
- It leaves out the instruction to label the plant.
- It invents online registration and early check-in.
- The revised version preserves only the information from the source brief.
- Model ratings need checking against the source and human observations.
